## Title

Project Ascent

## Tagline (one line)

A climber, a rotten tower, and levels that re-roll every time you die.

## Short description (the card blurb, about 150 characters)

A hard 2D platformer. 40 levels, 8 bosses, and most levels rebuild themselves every time you die. No memorising. Free.

## About

You fell out of the sky into the Spire: a tower outside time with no ground below it. One gate waits at the
top and needs 100 cores to open. Climb, collect, get out. PIP, the AI in your astronaut suit, keeps you
company. PIP is not nice about it.

**Levels you cannot memorise.** From level 6 on, every death rolls a brand-new level: new obstacles, new
pits, new timing. Every roll is checked by a bot before you see it, so it is always possible. It is just
never the same twice.

**40 levels, 8 acts, 8 bosses.** Cinder, Scrapyard, Freezer, Storm, Forge, Underworks, Rift and Core, each
with its own floors, traps and skyline. Every fifth level is a boss with its own rules: outrun a lava borer,
climb a flooding shaft, sneak past a warden's lantern, ground lightning rods in a storm, escape a collapse,
fight through a hive, outrun a black hole, and pass the final exam.

**A full moveset from the start.** Run, jump, wall-jump, dash, slide, ground pound, ledge grab, grapple and
a slash. Three health cells: spikes and drones cost one, lava costs all of them.

**Endless mode.** One run of stages that mix four biomes each. How far can you get?

**Fair, not kind.** Every hazard warns you before it hits. Deaths are instant and so are retries. Pick
STRONG and PIP roasts you harder every time you die; pick WEAK and PIP stays polite. Same levels either way.

**Make it yours.** Suit, visor and trim colours, gear, nine interface fonts, text size, colour-blind
correction, high-contrast platforms, extra jump grace, slower hazards, rebindable keys, Xbox, PlayStation
and Switch button prompts, and graphics presets that adapt to your PC.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0). No ads, no accounts, no tracking. If it ate an evening of your life, you can
buy the developer a coffee from the title screen; nothing is locked behind it.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Controller |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | A / D or arrows | Left stick |
| Jump | Space or W | A / Cross |
| Dash | Shift or J | X / Square |
| Slide | S or Ctrl | B / Circle |
| Slash | L or left mouse | RT |
| Grapple | E or K | RB |
| Restart | R | View / Select |
| Pause | Escape | Menu / Start |

Every key can be rebound in Settings. Tab shows the full list in game.

## Platforms and downloads

- **Browser:** play right here on the page (keyboard or controller).
- **Windows:** unzip and run `ProjectAscentLauncher.exe`.
- **macOS and Linux:** unzip and run the game.
- **Steam Deck:** works through the Windows or Linux build.
- Phones and consoles are not supported.
