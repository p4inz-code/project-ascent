## Title

Project Ascent

## Tagline (one line)

A climber, a rotten tower, and levels that re-roll every time you die.

## Short description (the card blurb, about 150 characters)

A hard 2D platformer with 40 levels and 8 bosses. Most levels rebuild themselves every time you die, so you can't memorise them. Free.

## About

You fell out of the sky into the Spire, a tower outside time with no ground below it. The only way out is a
gate at the top, and it needs 100 cores to open. PIP, the AI in your astronaut suit, comes along for the climb
and is not nice about it.

**Levels you cannot memorise.** From level 6 on, every death rolls a new level with different obstacles, pits
and timing. A bot plays each roll before you see it, so every level you get can be beaten. You just never get
the same one twice.

**40 levels, 8 acts, 8 bosses.** Cinder, Scrapyard, Freezer, Storm, Forge, Underworks, Rift and Core each have
their own floors, traps and skyline. Every fifth level is a boss with its own rules. You outrun a lava borer,
climb a flooding shaft, sneak past a warden's lantern, ground lightning rods in a storm, escape a collapse,
fight through a hive, outrun a black hole, and finally pass the exam.

**The whole moveset from the start.** Run, jump, wall-jump, dash, slide, ground pound, ledge grab, grapple and
slash. You have three health cells. Spikes and drones take one, and lava takes all three.

**Endless mode.** One long run where every stage mixes four biomes. See how far you get.

**Hard, with fair warning.** Every hazard warns you before it hits, and death and retry are both instant. On
STRONG, PIP roasts you harder with every death. On WEAK, PIP stays polite. The levels are the same either way.

**Make it yours.** Change the suit, visor and trim colours and the gear. Pick from nine interface fonts and set
the text size. For accessibility there is colour-blind correction, high-contrast platforms, extra jump grace
and slower hazards. Every key can be rebound, button prompts match Xbox, PlayStation or Switch pads, and the
graphics presets adjust to your PC.

The game is free and open source (GPL-3.0). It has no ads and no accounts, and it does not track you. If it ate
an evening of your life, you can buy the developer a coffee from the title screen. Nothing in the game is
locked behind it.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Controller |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | A / D or arrows | Left stick |
| Jump | Space or W | A / Cross |
| Dash | Shift or J | X / Square |
| Slide | S or Ctrl | B / Circle |
| Slash | L or left mouse | RT |
| Grapple | E or K | RB |
| Restart | R | View / Select |
| Pause | Escape | Menu / Start |

You can rebind every key in Settings. Press Tab in game to see the full list.

## Platforms and downloads

- **Browser:** play right on the page with a keyboard or controller.
- **Windows:** unzip and run `ProjectAscentLauncher.exe`. From version 0.18 the game checks once a day for a
  new version and updates itself when you say so. You can turn that off in Settings.
- **macOS and Linux:** unzip and run the game.
- **Steam Deck:** use the Windows or Linux build.
- **Requirements:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), about 250 MB of memory and 130 MB of disk space. Nothing else to
  install. If your PC cannot run the download, play it in the browser.
- Phones and consoles are not supported.
