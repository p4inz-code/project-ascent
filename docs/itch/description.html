A climber, a rotten tower, and levels that re-roll every time you die.

You fell out of the sky into the Spire, a tower outside time with no ground below it. The only way out is a gate at the top, and it needs 100 cores to open. PIP, the AI in your astronaut suit, comes along for the climb and is not nice about it.

Levels you cannot memorise

From level 6 on, every death rolls a new level with different obstacles, pits and timing. A bot plays each roll before you see it, so every level you get can be beaten. You just never get the same one twice.

40 levels, 8 acts, 8 bosses

Cinder, Scrapyard, Freezer, Storm, Forge, Underworks, Rift and Core each have their own floors, traps and skyline. Every fifth level is a boss with its own rules:

  • outrun a lava borer across a burning lake
  • climb a flooding shaft before the water reaches you
  • sneak past a warden's lantern
  • ground eight lightning rods in a storm
  • escape a collapsing forge, fight through a hive, outrun a black hole
  • and pass the final exam

The whole moveset from the first second

Run, jump, wall-jump, dash, slide, ground pound, ledge grab, grapple and slash. You have three health cells. Spikes and drones take one, and lava takes all three.

Hard, with fair warning

Every hazard warns you before it hits, and death and retry are both instant. On STRONG, PIP roasts you harder with every death. On WEAK, PIP stays polite. The levels are the same either way.

Endless

One long run where every stage mixes four biomes. See how far you get.

Make it yours

Change the suit, visor and trim colours and the gear. Pick from nine interface fonts and set the text size. For accessibility there is colour-blind correction, high-contrast platforms, extra jump grace and slower hazards. Every key can be rebound, button prompts match Xbox, PlayStation or Switch pads, and the graphics presets adjust to your PC.

Play

  • In your browser: right here on this page. Click the game once so it gets your keyboard.
  • Windows: download, unzip, and run ProjectAscentLauncher.exe.
  • macOS and Linux: download, unzip, and run the game.
  • Steam Deck: use the Windows or Linux build.

The game is free and open source (GPL-3.0). It has no ads and no accounts, and it does not track you. If it ate an evening of your life, you can buy the developer a coffee from the title screen. Nothing in the game is locked behind it.
