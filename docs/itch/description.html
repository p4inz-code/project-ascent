A climber, a rotten tower, and levels that re-roll every time you die.

You fell out of the sky into the Spire: a tower outside time with no ground below it. One gate waits at the top and needs 100 cores to open. Climb, collect, get out. PIP, the AI in your astronaut suit, keeps you company. PIP is not nice about it.

Levels you cannot memorise

From level 6 on, every death rolls a brand-new level: new obstacles, new pits, new timing. Every roll is checked by a bot before you see it, so it is always possible. It is just never the same twice.

40 levels, 8 acts, 8 bosses

Cinder, Scrapyard, Freezer, Storm, Forge, Underworks, Rift and Core, each with its own floors, traps and skyline. Every fifth level is a boss with its own rules:

  • outrun a lava borer across a burning lake
  • climb a flooding shaft before the water reaches you
  • sneak past a warden's lantern
  • ground eight lightning rods in a storm
  • escape a collapsing forge, fight through a hive, outrun a black hole
  • and pass the final exam

A full moveset from the first second

Run, jump, wall-jump, dash, slide, ground pound, ledge grab, grapple and a slash. Three health cells: spikes and drones cost one, lava costs all of them.

Fair, not kind

Every hazard warns you before it hits. Deaths are instant, and so are retries. Pick STRONG and PIP roasts you harder every time you die. Pick WEAK and PIP stays polite. Same levels either way.

Endless

One run of stages that mix four biomes each. How far can you get?

Make it yours

Suit, visor and trim colours, gear, nine interface fonts, text size, colour-blind correction, high-contrast platforms, extra jump grace, slower hazards, rebindable keys, Xbox / PlayStation / Switch button prompts, and graphics presets that adapt to your PC.
