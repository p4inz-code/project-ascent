# After v0.17.0: the last release from our side

**Planned on 2026-11-17: the full plan, with the owner's decisions and the phase order, is
[PLAN_v1.0.0.md](PLAN_v1.0.0.md).** The list below is the starting list it was built from.

Agreed with the owner on 2026-11-17. The current release (v0.17.0, itch.io launch) goes out first. After it,
there is **one more release, the last one from our side**, and it is **planned in depth before any work
starts**. It is the version bump to **v1.0.0**.

## Already on the list for that planning session

- **Launcher upgrade:** a proper in-game auto-updater. On start it checks for a new release, downloads it with
  a progress bar, verifies it, and applies it on restart (the owner's Kinoz app is the model; get its repo link).
- **Moves with purpose:** every verb required somewhere. Dash for gaps too wide to jump, double jump for ledges
  too high with no wall, wall-jump for shafts, slide under crushers, ground pound through cracked floors,
  grapple over voids. Each act mixes in the verbs it has taught.
- **Boss wall sections:** the owner's drawing (two or three tall walls with obstacles, zig-zag jumping), placed
  just before and just after boss levels; more patterns to come from the owner as drawings.
- **Cinematic story:** camera moves, parallax, effects and layered sound for the opening film.
- **Level 15 rework:** longer and harder (a second Warden, fewer hiding spots), with its own proof.
- **Difficulty toward the design targets:** tighter obstacles (the 5-frame minimum windows set the floor today).
- **Studio projects page:** real pitch lines and links for Kanvaz, Kinoz and Glint.
- **Marketing:** a short trailer and GIFs for itch, X and the GitHub page.

## Rules that carry over

Nothing ships unseen in the real game; every fix gets a test with a control; one Godot process at a time;
the full audit on a snapshot before the release; tag and publish only with the owner's go-ahead.
