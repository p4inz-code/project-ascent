## Rules
- Mock the PLAY in a fun, teasing way. Never the person.
- Swears are allowed from tier 4 on (fuck, shit, ass, damn, hell).
- BANNED outright (a CI scan will fail the build on any of these): motherfucker, son of a bitch, any mother / family / parent reference, slurs, gendered insults (bitch, cunt), bastard, real people, self-harm.
- Placeholders: {n} = deaths this level session, {pct} = progress at death.
- Tiers escalate with deaths per level session (reset on clear or a long break). Thresholds shift down on harder levels. No two lines within about 4 s, no repeat within about 20 deaths, a line never delays the respawn.
- Tier 5 keeps climbing forever (owner decision: no pressure-valve line, no hints in any level).
